Photometry lab

Turn a night of Seestar frames into a light curve. Your device does all the work: the frames never leave it, and nothing needs installing. Built for the Seestar S30 Pro and S50, and works with most FITS frames from other telescopes.

1

Load your frames

Choose the individual FITS frames from one night, usually several hundred. On the Seestar they are in the session folder ending in _sub. Use the light frames, not the stacked image.

No frames yet?

Run the whole pipeline on simulated Seestar frames of the real HD 189733 star field. A transit is planted at a known depth and time, along with clouds, a satellite trail and field rotation, so you can see what the lab finds.

Light curve

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, tap any point for its details. Use Select to drag a box around points to exclude, and Measure to read off the time and brightness between two points.

Fit

Is the noise random?

Scatter of the residuals as points are averaged into bigger bins. Pure random noise follows the dashed line. Lying above it means slow drifts (red noise) that make the result less certain than it looks.

Comparison stars

The light curve divides the target by the sum of the ticked stars. A good comparison star is steady; its own scatter is shown.
